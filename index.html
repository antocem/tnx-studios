The human part

Technology finds the signal.
People make it matter.

Intelligence tells us who’s worth calling and when. Everything after that is still people: the relationship, the creative judgement, the negotiation, the story. Strategy, brand and final approval stay with your team throughout.

Data — performance, overlap and cost per result

Creativity & storytelling — the brief, the concept, the cut
Judgement & negotiation — knowing when a number isn’t the answer
