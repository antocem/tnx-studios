The agreement

Two campaigns first.
Then we talk.

You run two paid campaigns with us before anything is signed. That is deliberate. It lets you see the briefs we write, the edits our studio sends back, how fast we answer and what we actually pay, while you stay completely free.

If it works for both sides, we put a partnership in writing. If it does not, you keep the fees and walk.

What we commit to you

  • You get first refusal on any brief that matches your category, ahead of the rest of the roster.
  • We negotiate upward on your behalf, using performance from your own campaigns rather than a guess.
  • We pitch you actively into accounts you would not reach, by name, not as a generic roster.
  • You keep every campaign asset we produce, in a version cut for your own grid.

What it does not mean

  • You are never obliged to accept a campaign. Passing costs you nothing and changes nothing.
  • You keep posting whatever you want outside brand work. Your channel stays yours entirely.
  • It starts after two paid campaigns, not before. You judge us on real work first.
  • Partnerships you already hold are carved out when we sign, and the term is fixed and written down with a clear exit.
